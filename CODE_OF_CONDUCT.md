# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

This repository is a place where anyone can ask questions and report problems, regardless of experience, background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Critique the problem, not the person.
- Keep threads on topic and use the right category or template.
- Respect privacy: no customer data, tokens or personal information.
- Accept that maintainers decide on scope and priorities.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or threats.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Spam, advertising or repeated off-topic posts.
- Deliberately disruptive or bad-faith reports.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock threads and block accounts. Report problems to [info@tegrasystems.nl](mailto:info@tegrasystems.nl). Reports are handled confidentially.

This code of conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
